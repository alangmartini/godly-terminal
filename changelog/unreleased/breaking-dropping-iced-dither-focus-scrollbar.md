### Added
- **Gradient dithering** — Triangle noise dithering in the SDF shader eliminates visible color banding on dark gradients (sidebar, tab bar backgrounds)
- **Window focus/unfocus dimming** — Subtle 15% dark overlay when the window loses focus, matching professional app behavior (VS Code, Zed)
- **Scrollbar hover proximity** — Scrollbar widens (6→10px) and brightens as the mouse approaches the right edge of the terminal area, with smooth quadratic easing
